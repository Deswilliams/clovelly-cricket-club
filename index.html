Our Club

Local cricket with deep Clovelly roots.

Founded in 1986, Clovelly Cricket Club was built on local friendships, community spirit and a strong connection to Clovelly's beach culture. The club brings together players of different ages and abilities in a relaxed, inclusive cricket environment.
