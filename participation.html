How to read the figures The default order is most innings batted first, then most Top 5 innings, with remaining ties alphabetical. Bowling does not affect this ranking.

% batted = matches in which the player batted ÷ matches played. Each order band shows its innings count and percentage of all innings batted: Top 5, middle order 6–8, and bottom order 9–11. A dash means there is no denominator yet. Percentages are rounded to whole numbers.

Played means the player actually batted or bowled at least one ball. Players listed without batting or bowling are not counted as having played. Fill in players whose names are withheld are excluded.

Overs use cricket notation: 2.3 is two overs and three balls. Bowling is N/A for “Not a bowler”. These figures show involvement; they are not a player rating.
