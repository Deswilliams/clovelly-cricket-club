How to read these figures

How to read the figures The default order is most innings batted first, then most Top 5 innings, then most middle-order innings (6–8), with remaining ties alphabetical. Each grade is counted and ranked separately. Players who fill in may appear in both grades. Bowling does not affect this ranking. The far-left Rank column keeps each player’s batting opportunity rank when other columns are sorted; 1 is the highest.

% batted = matches in which the player batted ÷ matches played. Each order band shows its innings count and percentage of all innings batted: Top 5, middle order 6–8, and bottom order 9–11. A dash means there is no denominator yet. Percentages are rounded to whole numbers. Batting-order columns sort by innings count.

Played means the player actually batted or bowled at least one ball. Players listed without batting or bowling are not counted as having played. Fill in players whose names are withheld are excluded.

Overs use cricket notation: 2.3 is two overs and three balls. Games bowled and overs are N/A for “Not a bowler”. Wicket totals show recorded wickets for every player. These figures show involvement; they are not a player rating.

This season’s batting average and wickets use the completed matches for the selected grade listed below. Batting average = runs ÷ dismissals; a dash means no dismissals or incomplete data. Career figures come from PlayCricket and may include other clubs. A dash in career figures means no average or unavailable data. These statistics do not affect the opportunity ranking.
