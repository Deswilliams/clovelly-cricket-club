Our approach

Fair opportunity across the season

Clovelly Second Grade aims to give players reasonable opportunities to contribute, while maintaining a balanced and competitive team. Batting position, bowling role, performance and match conditions all influence how those opportunities arise.

Fair opportunity does not mean everyone bats and bowls equally. We track involvement over the season to support clear, consistent conversations within the team.

Three bowling roles

Front line bowler
Bowling is a regular part of the player’s role.
Fill in bowler
May bowl when team balance or match conditions call for it.
Not a bowler
Bowling opportunity is shown as N/A.

Second Grade participation dashboard

Completed scorecards update the figures when you open the dashboard. Anyone can view the participation figures. Only the club administrator can change bowling categories.
